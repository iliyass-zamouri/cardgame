1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how ShadowHand (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our multiplayer card game on mobile devices.

Package / app identifier: com.hailsom.shadowhand

Contact: privacy@hailsom.com

2. Information we collect

Depending how you play, we may collect:

  • Account & profile data. Guest display name and username; if you sign in with Google, your Google account identifier and any name Google provides for account linking.
  • Device identifiers. An install-scoped guest device ID stored on your device, plus basic device metadata you send during guest sign-in (for example platform and model).
  • Network data. IP address observed by our servers when you authenticate or play (used to bind/resume guest sessions and operate the service).
  • Gameplay data. Room codes, match state, rankings, and in-game actions needed to run multiplayer sessions.
  • Local preferences. Settings stored on your device (for example language, age confirmation, and signed-in session / profile cache including a session token).
  • Push notification tokens. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens and notification preference flags if you enable push.
  • Purchase / monetization data. In-app purchase product identifiers and transaction identifiers processed via platform billing and RevenueCat to grant virtual currency or items. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Analytics & crash data. Firebase Analytics events (for example login, match start/end, shop actions) and Firebase Crashlytics stack traces / device metadata for stability.
  • Advertising data. When ads are shown, Google AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data as described in their policies. In the EEA/UK we request consent via Google’s UMP where required.

We do not ask for your phone contacts, photos, or precise GPS location for core gameplay. In-game balances are virtual currency only and have no cash value.

3. How we use information

  • Create and restore guest or Google-linked accounts
  • Authenticate API and multiplayer sessions (session tokens)
  • Display your name to other players in matches
  • Operate matchmaking, private rooms, and live game sessions
  • Grant and protect virtual currency / cosmetics purchases
  • Send optional push notifications you choose (invites, social, etc.)
  • Measure usage, improve stability, and prevent abuse
  • Show ads that help keep the App available
  • Comply with legal obligations when required

4. How we share information

We may share information with:

  • Other players — your display name and in-game status are visible during a match.
  • Service providers — hosting, databases, and infrastructure needed to run the App.
  • Google — Google Sign-In, AdMob, Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging). See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and entitlement status. See their privacy documentation.
  • Legal / safety — if required by law, or to protect users, our rights, or the integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising

The App may show interstitial and rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Ad partners may use cookies, advertising IDs, or similar technologies. Where required (EEA/UK), we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. You can also limit ad personalization in device settings.

6. Data retention

Account and gameplay-related records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to operate, secure, and improve the App, unless a longer period is required by law. Local profile and preference data remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

7. Children’s privacy

ShadowHand is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Play as a guest without linking a Google account
  • Sign out from Settings
  • Delete your account in Settings — this removes your server profile and related data (cascading deletes), and clears the local session on the device
  • Clear local app data or uninstall to remove on-device storage
  • Email privacy@hailsom.com for access, correction, or deletion help if in-app delete is unavailable

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws (for example GDPR or CCPA). Contact us to exercise those rights.

See also our Terms of Service.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use the App accordingly.

10. International users

Our servers or providers may process data in countries other than yours. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations as needed to provide the service.
